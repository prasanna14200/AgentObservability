# Arize Phoenix — Competitive Research Record

**Research date:** 13 August 2026

## What it is
Arize Phoenix is an AI observability and evaluation platform with tracing, evaluations, experiments, agent-oriented analysis and OpenTelemetry-related workflows.

## Verified evidence
The Phoenix repository identifies `phoenix-evals` with Elastic-2.0 licensing. Recent releases show agent-oriented online trace evaluations, token charts, trace/span export and experiment evaluation features.

## Relevance to AgentSentinel
Phoenix is an important comparison point for tracing, evaluation, agent observability and operational analysis.

## Important positioning
Do not reduce Phoenix to only embedding drift or claim that it has no agent evaluation.

AgentSentinel focuses on behavioral anomaly detection combined with runtime safety decisions and pre-tool-call intervention.
