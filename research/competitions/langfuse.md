# Langfuse — Competitive Research Record

**Research date:** 13 August 2026

## What it is
Langfuse is an open-source LLM/agent observability platform covering tracing/observability, evaluation, prompt management and related workflows.

## Verified license
The official repository states that content outside specified `ee` directories is MIT Expat licensed; enterprise portions are separately licensed.

## Relevance to AgentSentinel
Langfuse is a strong reference for the observability and evaluation layer.

## Important positioning
Do not claim Langfuse has no anomaly or safety-related capabilities without feature-by-feature verification.

AgentSentinel is not intended to replace Langfuse. Its target architecture adds a behavioral safety control plane around agent execution.
